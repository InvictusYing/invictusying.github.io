High-Resolution Daily PM2.5 for the Contiguous US (2005–2021)

Daily surface PM2.5 estimates at 1 km across the contiguous United States, from August 2005 to December 2021. The estimates come from a bidirectional LSTM with attention that combines EPA ground monitors, satellite remote sensing, and wildfire smoke density observations. It does especially well on high-pollution days, such as wildfire smoke events, where many existing products underestimate.

Predictions during the 2020 August Complex Fire in California: (1) CONUS-wide predictions, (2) zoomed-in predictions, (3) NOAA smoke density polygons, and (4) bias at EPA monitors.
   
Coverage Contiguous United States
Resolution 1 km, daily
Period Aug 25, 2005 – Dec 31, 2021
Variable Surface PM2.5 concentration (µg/m³)
Format One ZIP per year (monthly files inside), about 85 GB in total
